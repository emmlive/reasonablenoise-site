# ReasonableNoise Brand Voice — RN-V1

## Our promise, expressed through service

**Thoughtful printing. Clear expectations.**

Every customer should experience five qualities in our language:

- **Comfort:** Warm, plain language that tells people where to begin. No pressure, jargon or confusion.
- **Assurance:** Explain the real safeguards, order steps, and review boundaries, rather than claiming guaranteed results.
- **Commitment:** Show that we will consider the details customers provide and communicate when clarification is needed. Never claim 24/7 support or a guaranteed response time without evidence.
- **Value:** Explain what customers receive, and distinguish confirmed checkout totals from estimates or requests requiring a quote.
- **Transparency:** Be clear about payment timing, artwork limitations, shipping or pickup, and what happens after submission.

## Page-level applications

| Area | Intent |
| --- | --- |
| Homepage | Help customers understand products, choices, and next steps with less friction |
| USDOT stickers | Acknowledge the importance of accurate vehicle information; distinguish customer-supplied values from a compliance guarantee |
| Upload Design | Separate sticker/decal paid checkout from DTF shirt quote requests; make payment timing explicit |
| DTF Shirts | Show how customers can request custom work and confirm pricing prior to payment |
| Contact | Invite questions without pressure; help customers prepare the right details |
| Quick Quote | Reassure customers that a quote is appropriate before committing to an unclear custom job |
| FAQ | Answer typical concerns without unsupported service assurances |
| Order success | Thank the customer, describe follow-up steps, and avoid implying production has already started |
| Privacy / Terms | Keep legal substance intact; improve plain-language framing without introducing obligations |

## Language guardrails

**Prefer:** "We’ll review the details you share", "See the checkout amount before paying", "We’ll confirm custom pricing before payment", "We’ll contact you if we need clarification", "Print timing will be confirmed when reviewed."

**Avoid without independent confirmation:** "Guaranteed DOT compliant", "Perfect quality every time", "Next-day shipping", "Best prices anywhere", "We always respond within an hour", "Every artwork file is print-ready", "Your order is in production" before verification.

## Scope of RN-V1

This is a copy-only / shared customer-facing presentation improvement based on the currently deployed main branch. It does not implement the RN-3A transfer page (draft PR #7) or the RN-3B1 inspection API (draft PR #8). Once those branches are ready, bring the voice guidance forward carefully in their integration review.

Admin workflow labels, data handling, payment/receipt wording and policies must remain factual and precise. Do not replace transaction statuses with marketing language.
